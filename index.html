How It Works

Four steps from bottle
to bragging rights

1
📸
Scan any bottle
Camera, barcode, or manual search. AI identifies the drink with a confidence score and fetches tasting notes from verified sources.
2
👥
Set up your game
Add players, choose your mode (pass-the-phone or multi-device), set the timer, and optionally tag the game to a Cellar Club season.
3
🎯
Guess the notes
Take turns naming tasting notes. Each guess is scored instantly — correct, specific, or wrong. Other players can't see what you've guessed.
4
📊
See the reveal
All tasting notes uncovered with sources. Your Palate Profile updates automatically. Share results or play again.
Features

Everything you need to know

📸
AI Bottle Scan
Point your camera at any wine, beer, or spirits label. Our AI cross-references producer databases and returns verified tasting notes in seconds.
🏆
Cellar Club Leagues
Create a group with mates, set a season end date, and run a proper league. Points accumulate across games — not just one bottle.
Pro
🧬
Palate Profile
Every guess feeds your tasting DNA. See accuracy by flavour category, strengths and weaknesses, and how you're improving over time.
🎯
Precision Scoring
Generic correct = +1. Name the specific variant — like "Rainier cherry" instead of just "cherry" — and earn +2. Producer notes pay double.
📱
Pass-the-Phone & Multi-Device
Hand the phone around the table, or everyone plays on their own device simultaneously. Works offline too.
🛑
Opt-Out Mechanic
Lock in your score at any point and watch others risk it. Knowing when to stop adds real strategy — not just taste knowledge.
