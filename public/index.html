How It Works

Four steps from bottle
to bragging rights

1
📸
Scan any bottle
Camera, barcode, or search. We recognise the bottle and pull its tasting notes from trusted sources.
2
👥
Gather the table
Add players, pass one phone around or play on your own devices, set the timer, and tag the game to your Cellar Club season.
3
🎯
Guess the notes
Take turns naming what you taste. Every guess is scored on the spot. Nobody sees your answers until the reveal.
4
🏆
See the reveal
Every note uncovered, with sources. Your Palate Profile updates itself. Share the results, or save the bottle for next time.
Features

Made for the whole table

📸
Bottle Scan
Point your camera at any wine, beer, or spirits label. We cross-check producer data and return verified tasting notes in seconds.
🏆
Cellar Club Leagues
Start a club with your mates, set a season end date, and run a proper league with a table everyone can see.
Pro
🧬
Palate Profile

"Cherry" scores +1. "Rainier cherry" scores +2. Producer notes pay double. Be specific, be rewarded.

Pass-the-Phone & Multi-Device
Hand one phone around the table, or everyone plays on their own. Bottles you've scanned work offline too.

Lock In Your Score
Happy with your haul? Lock it in any time, and let everyone else keep hunting for notes.
